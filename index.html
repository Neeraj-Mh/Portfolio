How I take AI from idea to production

  1. Discover & prioritise

    Score use cases on value, feasibility and risk, build the business case with stakeholders and own the AI backlog of epics and acceptance criteria.

  2. Design the system

    Choose the pattern (autonomous agents, multi-agent orchestration, RAG, document intelligence) and define tool-calling API schemas into Core Banking and microservices.

  3. Guardrail it

    HITL checkpoints, deterministic fallbacks, prompt standards and model-risk controls mapped to credit policy, privacy and responsible lending.

  4. Evaluate

    Golden test datasets and metrics such as extraction accuracy, precision and recall, hallucination rate and straight-through-processing rate; UAT for AI outputs.

  5. Ship & monitor

    Move from PoC to pilot to production with data science and engineering, then track performance and exceptions on live dashboards.
